How an answer is made

  1. Shared prefix. The template, images and state are computed once by a seed branch; every other branch reuses that KV cache.
  2. Two orders. Options are scored as given and rotated by n/2; probabilities map back to the original options and average.
  3. Gate. If the two orders pick different options (3+ options), a second pass adds the n/4 and 3n/4 rotations.
  4. Nine bins. Two-option questions also get “rate option (A) from 1 to 9”; the expected digit becomes a probability that is blended in.

One forward step per branch, read at the answer position. Nothing is generated.
